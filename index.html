zGallery for Mac

zGallery is the elegant image and photo viewer that lets you browse, view, and edit files directly from your folders and disks. Simple to use, yet equipped with every essential power tool.

Universal Format Support

Displays virtually every common image format, including thumbnails for all file types—even video frames—for seamless browsing.

Effortless Navigation

Tree panel for folder search and exploration, with intuitive zooming, fullscreen mode, and slideshows to match your workflow.

Built-in File Management

Copy, move, delete, and organize files just like Finder, with full drag-and-drop and copy-paste support.

Precise Image Editing

Rotate, flip, resize, convert formats, and more—essential tools right at your fingertips, no extra apps needed.

Instant Wallpaper Setup

Set any image as your desktop background in one click, perfectly integrated with macOS.

How It Works

Step 1: Open Your Folder

Launch zGallery and point it to any folder or disk. Thumbnails load instantly for every image and supported file.

Step 2: Browse and Zoom

Navigate the tree panel, zoom in or out, switch to fullscreen, or start a slideshow with fluid controls.

Step 3: Edit and Manage

Rotate, resize, or convert on the fly. Copy, move, or delete files using familiar Finder gestures.

Step 4: Customize Your Desktop

Right-click any image to set it as wallpaper—done.
